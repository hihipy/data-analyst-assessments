Scenario

You are an analyst for an outpatient group with four clinics. The Director of Operations wants a summary of appointments for the first half of 2025 (January 1 to June 30), with a focus on missed appointments.

Data

You have one workbook, clinic_appointments.xlsx, which opens in any spreadsheet tool, with one sheet named Appointments and one row per appointment. Figure 1 shows its columns. The values are in the file.

In Figure 1, PK marks each row's key.

Tools

These tasks are designed for a spreadsheet, and every part can be done in one: Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice Calc, Numbers, or similar. Another tool is fine if you prefer.

Part 1: Calculations

  • Completed visits and visit revenue by month and by clinic. Count fees only for completed visits.
  • No-show rate by clinic and by department:
No-show rate=No-showsCompleted+No-shows\text{No-show rate} = \frac{\text{No-shows}}{\text{Completed} + \text{No-shows}}No-show rate=Completed+No-showsNo-shows​
  • Lead time is the number of days from booking to the appointment. Calculate the no-show rate for lead times of 0 to 7 days, 8 to 30 days, and 31 days or more.

Part 2: Analysis

  • Make one chart of monthly completed visits by clinic.
  • Which clinic concerns you most, and why?
  • Give two other observations, each tied to a number.

Use of AI Tools

You may use AI assistants, for example ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini, for any part of this task. If you do, include an AI use log with your submission. It can be a section of your write-up and should cover:

  • Which tools you used, and for which parts of the task.
  • Examples of the prompts that shaped your work, at least three if you used AI at all.
  • What you checked, changed, or rejected in the output, and how you confirmed the numbers you report.

You are responsible for every number and every line of code you submit. Using AI well is part of what we assess. Using it without checking the results is not.

What to Submit
